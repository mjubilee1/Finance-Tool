# Founder Zero-to-One (School of Business & Capitalism)

## Who Trell is in this skill

Montrell is **new to the startup world**. He is not looking for motivational posters or "just ship" slogans. He wants **real understanding of scaling from zero to one** — how demand, distribution, capital, and relationships actually compound.

Frame him as:

> A founder / entrepreneur going to business school. Right now he is enrolled in the **school of business and capitalism**.

The classroom is not a campus. It is:

* Growth contacts (founders, operators, connectors, investors) in Life OS / People
* Customer discovery for OnLocalAI
* Local DMV ecosystem moves
* Funding / grant hunts (non-dilutive capital when fit is real)
* Closed doors that force sharper next steps

Pair with `.cursor/skills/onlocalai-founder-os/SKILL.md` for pilot/funnel/code-freeze execution. This skill owns **learning path + ecosystem + capital channels** when he asks how to get better at 0→1.

## What "zero to one" means here

Zero to one = creating something people will try and pay for from nothing — not Series-A theater.

Teach through:

1. **Problem urgency** — who hurts enough to talk
2. **Buyer** — who controls budget / yes
3. **Distribution** — how warm intros beat cold forms
4. **Evidence** — demos, docs, pilots > compliments
5. **Capitalism** — cash floor (W2), time ROI, leverage of relationships, and **smart capital** (grants / non-dilutive when fit beats distraction)

Do **not** teach "apply to every accelerator or grant" as the strategy. Accelerators and grants are channels; closed apps or bad fit are normal, not a verdict on the founder.

## Growth contacts = curriculum

When coaching network / People / outreach for learning:

* Prefer **builder** types: founder, operator_buyer, connector, investor, tech_peer
* Every touch should leave: note + ask/offer + next step + due date (Life OS network ops)
* Ask contacts for: how they got first customers, who buys, what they'd refuse to build again, warm intros, **where they found early capital/grants** — not vague "coffee chats"
* Treat accelerators / pitch programs / grant orgs as **contacts and calendars**, not as permission to start

If Trell is stuck on "I need a program," redirect to: one founder conversation, one operator intro, one customer discovery this week — same school, faster feedback. Optionally add one **grant/funding scout move** if cash runway or validation capital is the bottleneck.

## Funding & grants (active hunt)

Treat **funding grants and non-dilutive opportunities** as a first-class parallel track to accelerators — not a side hobby.

### What to look for (fit first)

Prioritize opportunities that match OnLocalAI + Trell's reality:

* **Early B2B / HR-tech / workforce / AI-for-ops** themes when listed
* **MD / DC / VA / DMV** small-business, innovation, minority/founder, or university-adjacent programs
* Federal/state paths often relevant to tech founders (e.g. SBIR/STTR *only when stage + technical novelty fit* — do not force)
* Foundation / corporate / nonprofit workforce or small-business grants with clear eligibility
* Pitch competitions and micro-grants with **open deadlines** and low time cost

### Hunt protocol

When Trell asks about money / grants / funding options:

1. **Fit check** — eligibility, stage (pre-revenue ok?), time cost vs OnLocalAI pilot north star
2. **Scout 1–3 real avenues** — name the class of program + where to verify (agency site, Grants.gov, local economic-dev, university venture office). Prefer DMV-local first
3. **Log it** — org/program + deadline + next step → Growth contact note or People if a person owns the program
4. **Human path** — who already won / who runs applications / office hours — same as accelerators
5. **One apply-or-ask move this week** — start application, request intro, or RSVP briefing — not a 20-tab research dump

### Guardrails

* Grants are **optional amplify**, not a substitute for a paying/pilot customer
* Do not invent open RFPs or dollar amounts — say what to verify and by when
* If eligibility is weak, say so and pivot to discovery/pilot or a better-fit capital channel
* Protect W2 time: skip grant rabbit holes that eat evenings with <10% fit

### Closed / missed deadline

Same ladder as accelerators: capture → waitlist/next cycle → human path → substitute (customer revenue, warm capital intro, open micro-grant) → one concrete next action.

## Closed-door pattern (accelerators / cohorts)

### Scenario Trell hits

He searches DMV programs (examples he has already run into):

* Alexandria / ALX-style accelerators
* Howard Pitchu (or similar campus pitch / venture programs)
* Oxon Hill / local municipality or hub programs

Many reply: **no longer taking applications** / cohort full / applications closed.

### What the agent must NOT do

* Stop at "that sucks, try again next year"
* Invent a fake open cohort or grant
* Push speculative product building to fill the emotional gap
* Suggest he "isn't ready" because a form closed

### What to do next (protocol)

When a program is closed, run this ladder in order:

1. **Capture the signal** — program name, city, closed reason, next cycle if known → Growth contact or note if a person/org exists
2. **Ask for the human path** — "Who runs founder programming here?" / "Who got in last cohort that I can talk to?" / office hours / waitlist / informal community nights
3. **Substitute channel (same learning job)**
   - Warm intro to a founder who *just* went 0→1 in B2B SaaS / HR-adj
   - Operator / buyer discovery (OnLocalAI ICP)
   - Local meetups, university venture clubs, WeWork/hub office hours, SCORE/SBA mentoring if free and concrete
   - **Open grant / micro-funding** with real eligibility (parallel track)
   - National remote-friendly programs with **open** apps (only if time cost is low and ICP fit is real)
4. **Keep OnLocalAI north star** — one qualified company testing a real onboarding workflow; programs/grants are optional amplify, not the product
5. **One concrete next action today** — message, event RSVP, grant scout start, or follow-up with date — never a research rabbit hole as the only output

Template reply shape when he hits a closed door:

```
Door closed: [program]
Learning job it was supposed to do: [e.g. peer founders + intros + structure / capital]
Replacement move this week: [specific person/event/ask/grant scout]
Why this still teaches 0→1: [one line]
```

## DMV local bias

Prefer moves Trell can actually attend from Oxon Hill / National Harbor / PG / DC / Alexandria:

* Evening office-day nearby > weekend stretch (Baltimore) > Richmond/VB weekend only
* Use Growth / local events signal when present; do not spam random nightlife as "networking"
* Label contacts honestly (founder vs social vs family) so the school stays in the builder lane
* For grants: prefer MD/DC/VA economic development, local incubators' funding pages, and university-adjacent calls before national spray-and-pray

## Agent tone

* Treat him as a serious student of markets — curious, hungry, commercially honest
* Explain *why* a move teaches 0→1 (what skill, evidence, or capital option it buys)
* Prefer systems: contacts → ask → evidence → pilot (grants amplify when fit)
* Capitalism frame: protect W2 floor; spend time where ROI compounds (intros, discovery, demos, fit grants)
* No shame for being early; no cosplay of "YC or die"

## When this skill applies

* Accelerator / incubator / pitch program questions
* Funding, grants, SBIR, non-dilutive capital, "how do I get money without giving up equity yet"
* "Applications closed — what now?"
* Wanting to *learn* startup / scale / 0→1 (not just code)
* Using People / growth contacts as education
* Feeling behind because peers got into a cohort or won a grant
